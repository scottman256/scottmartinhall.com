My Favorite Video Games

1

Super Mario Galaxy 1/2

The Mario Galaxy games are the first truly polished 3D Mario games. They are an absolute blast to play. Super Mario 64 came first, then Sunshine disappointed. After that Nintendo released these stunning games on the Wii. Although Galaxy 2 is the better game, the original brought the idea and the style, so I put them both at #1.

2

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

The best Mario Kart game in the series, Mario Kart 8 is a joy to play. After playing it countless times starting with the Wii U version, I am still not bored of it, and take time to play it from time to time.

3

The Legend Of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

At my core, I am an adventurous guy, and Zelda Breath of the Wild is an adventurous game. I was truly immersed in the world, and had a lot of fun exploring. I'm not a big fan of the Zelda series overall, but this game is amazing.

4

Super Smash Bros Ultimate

Smash Bros Ultimate lands at 4 mostly because of the sheer amount of Nintendo content. A large amount of characters, stages, music, and an addicting single player adventure are the main reasons it is here on the list of my current favs. It's also fun to play with friends.

5

Super Mario 3D World

I was considering putting Super Mario Odyssey here, but I think I had more fun playing this one. The multiplayer mode lets you play with friends, and it actually works well. The extra worlds at the end are a nice bonus. It brings back the classic Mario Bros feel, but in 3D. It's also less weird than Odyssey.
